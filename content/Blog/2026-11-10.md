- Back to work after a three-month career break; spent more than half the time travelling, in Spain (to see the eclipse), Bosnia & Herzegovina (to explore the Via Dinarica and, inadvertently, the healthcare system) and Montenegro; feeling re-energised for lots of things including this blog
- Sad to hear of the [passing of Bob Jolliffe](https://www.facebook.com/dhis2/posts/it-is-with-deep-sadness-that-we-share-the-news-of-the-passing-of-our-dear-friend/1464966982294623/), a lovely, wise guru figure in the world of DHIS 2 implementation, who I was fortunate to meet and learn from; he will be missed
- I have been organising my many old notes in Obsidian, inspired by the indefatigable blogger Pablo Murad (https://pablomurad.com/an-obsidian-vault-that-lasts/); it is amazing what you can do but I sometimes feel I'm still just trying to recreate what I had with Tiddlywiki; I've also updated this blog to the latest version and am reorganising a lot of my stuff; apologies for any dead links, non-functional pages or RSS overload in the meantime; someone has actually read my blog (!) and reached out about a Zotero-Obsidian integration they are working on which I will look at when I can
- I have been working on the foundations of a new SENAITE configuration and deployment, this time for a group of labs; I'm using the [senaite.starter](https://github.com/senaite/senaite.starter) template (now open source), and creating templates in Proxmox so I can create throwaway clones to test things on
- Didn't make as much progress with book writing (principles of outbreak investigation) when I was off as I had expected (or promised) but I am now making progress every day  
- Wasn't using my cheap Alibaba (small form factor) computer much, so I bought an old Thinkpad and am using [Pop!_OS](https://system76.com/pop?srsltid=AYCX9vLtlEdBkfr6vDQKQYuIkIAoYONFav9OKYE4YmBVYXfPFIxB5kab) on that (had to upgrade firmware to address intermittent crashes); thinking of things I can use the other computer for (e.g. another Proxmox node, a media server for my old DVD rips and YouTube downloads, CCTV) 
- Have stopped using [Karakeep](https://karakeep.com/) after trying [FreshRSS](https://freshrss.org/index.html) with [Capy Reader](https://capyreader.com/) and finding it more convenient; also stopped using [Rustdesk](https://rustdesk.com/) as I don't need remote access to anyone's computer anymore
- No book list this year but read a few things while off/away: finished Neuromancer by William Gibson; also read The Hawkline Monster by Richard Brautigan, Orlando by Virginia Woolf, The Brothers Karamazov by Fyodor Dostoevsky, East of Eden by John Steinbeck and A History Of The World in 47 Borders by Jonn Elledge (all good); still working through Statistical Rethinking and have also started reading Networking for System Administrators by Michael W Lucas, and Fundamentals of Data Engineering by Joe Reis and Matt Housley.
