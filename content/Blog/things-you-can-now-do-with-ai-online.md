In no particular order - some require signup but all have some free options

- [AI Book Generator - Generate eBooks in One Click and Sell Them on Bookstores.](https://www.aibookgenerator.net/): could this write an overdue book on outbreak investigation? (asking for a friend)
- [AIcyclopedia | The largest AI encyclopedia](https://www.aicyclopedia.com/): list of free and commercial AI tools
- [AutoDraw](https://www.autodraw.com/): draw freehand and see matching icons
- [Awesome ChatGPT Prompts | This repo includes ChatGPT prompt curation to use ChatGPT better.](https://prompts.chat/): refine your timewasting by asking ChatGPT better questions ("prompts")
- [Best AI Writers for Students - a Comprehensive List | by A. Noah | Becoming Human: Artificial Intelligence Magazine](https://becominghuman.ai/best-ai-writers-for-students-a-comprehensive-list-8ceefc2695e2): list of affordable essay writing AI tools for cheating students (NB: there is now AI for detecting essays written by AI)
- [Business Name Generator - Powered by AI - Namelix](https://namelix.com/app/?keywords=Data%20extractor%20from%20electronic%20forms): could also be good for naming apps
- [ChatGPT Prompt Generator - a Hugging Face Space by merve](https://huggingface.co/spaces/merve/ChatGPT-prompt-generator): use AI to generate prompts for AI, e.g. *I want you to act as an epidemiologist. My first request is "I need help diagnosing an epidemic."*
- [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/chat): have a conversation with AI
- [DALL·E 2](https://openai.com/product/dall-e-2): try creating your own art work; you get some free credits
- [Digital AI Art Generator creates beautiful art | Coloromo](https://www.coloromo.com/coloromo-digital-art): not really free but quite impressive results; turn a boring photo of your bedroom into a Van Gogh (I did)
- [Dream by WOMBO](https://dream.ai/create): AI art again; you get one free image
- [Free Profile Picture Maker - Create an Awesome PFP Online](https://pfpmaker.com/): no AI available to this generation can make me photogenic unfortunately
- [Futurepedia - The Largest AI Tools Directory | Home](https://www.futurepedia.io/): also the largest AI tools directory
- [Have I Been Trained?](https://haveibeentrained.com/): check if any of your online photos were used to train generative art AI
- [Home - TheAIGeek.com](https://theaigeek.com/): another list of AI tools
- [Lensa - Prisma Labs](https://prisma-ai.com/lensa): mobile phone app for converting your selfies to AI art; has a free trial period
- [Nyx.gallery - AI-generated photography](https://nyx.gallery/): free AI-generated photos for you to use as you will (but attribution is always nice)
- [Palette - Colorize Photos](https://palette.fm/): does what it says, for free
- [Paper to HTML | Allen Institute for AI](https://papertohtml.org/): converts scientific papers in PDF format into HTML documents, using AI
- [Power and Weirdness: How to Use Bing AI - by Ethan Mollick](https://oneusefulthing.substack.com/p/power-and-weirdness-how-to-use-bing): you can now use AI in Bing
- [RTutor](http://rtutor.ai/): Shiny app using AI to convert your English text into R code
- [Recipes By AI](https://recipesby.ai/): generate random recipes based on the ingredients you specify
- [ResumeMaker.Online | Create a Professional Resume for Free](https://www.resumemaker.online/): CV creator, with AI-suggested bullet points
- [SmartyNames.com : Search Domain Names with Artificial Intelligence](https://smartynames.com/): let AI suggest domain names for your company website
- [Sona - Read This Twice](https://www.readthistwice.com/sona): not sure what to read next? Let AI help!
- [TLDR This - Article Summarizer & Online Text Summarizing Tool](https://tldrthis.com/): summarise any Web page in various ways; doesn't seem to work for technical articles
- [Top AI Tools - AiTopTools.com | AI Tools Directory](https://aitoptools.com/): another list of AI tools
- [Transcribe File](https://freesubtitles.ai/): automatically convert your ripped DVDs to subtitled versions
- [Will Robots Take My Job?](https://willrobotstakemyjob.com/): not the "use AI to find out if AI will take your job" I was hoping for, but including it for interest - epidemiologists seem safe so far
- [punchlines.ai :: Generate jokes with a GPT-based AI](https://punchlines.ai/): not hilarious, though I have seen ChatGPT producing some good jokes
- [Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai/): ask ChatGPT questions without any login
