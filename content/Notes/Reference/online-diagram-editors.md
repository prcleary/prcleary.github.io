Needing to draw an information system architecture so looking at my options.

- [Edotor — Your Favorite Online Graphviz Editor](https://edotor.net/) - Graphviz is incredible (once you learn it) and was what I started with a couple of decades ago
- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/) - love this one for quick "hand-drawn" diagrams
- [tldraw](https://www.tldraw.com/) - another good one for quick hand-drawn diagrams - new version in preparation available at [tldraw](https://beta.tldraw.com/) at the time of writing
- [Free Online Diagram Editor](https://www.diagrameditor.com/) - another yEd clone
- [Online FlowChart & Diagrams Editor - Mermaid Live Editor](https://mermaid.live) - check out the sample diagrams
- [Project planning example - Markwhen](https://markwhen.com/) - looks very good for Gantt charts
- [Railroad Diagram Generator](https://tabatkins.github.io/railroad-diagrams/generator.html) - not applicable here but looks good
- [diagrams.net](https://app.diagrams.net/) - superficially similar to yEd
- [yEd Live](https://www.yworks.com/yed-live/) - my favourite - have used the free Windows app version a lot

Recommend exporting as SVG (always) as this gives vector graphics quality and is compatible with HTML, Word, Powerpoint, etc.
